### A clearer picture of a world in motion.

RASD Technologies is building distributed radar for the places where sensing needs to be practical, affordable, and connected.

Our first product focuses on **drone detection**. **Vehicle analytics** comes next. The longer-term vision is a network of compact radar nodes that turns local observations into shared situational awareness.

### From RF to the operator.

| Hardware & RF | Signal processing | Software & systems |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Radar front ends, acquisition, and node design. | Turning raw I/Q into motion information, with tracking and classification in development. | Data pipelines, operator interfaces, and the foundations of a connected sensor network. |

We build across the stack because the quality of the final picture starts with the quality of the signal.

### Built on measurements.

Our **24 GHz bench platform is live**, with CW and FMCW acquisition and repeatable indoor human-motion measurements. Outdoor drone validation and production-node development are the next steps.

Measured results, modeled performance, and product targets are distinct. Our public work follows that distinction.
